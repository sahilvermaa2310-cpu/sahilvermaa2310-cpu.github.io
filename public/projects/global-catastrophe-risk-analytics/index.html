Global-Catastrophe-Risk-Analytics

Built an end-to-end catastrophe risk analytics platform using PostgreSQL, PostGIS, and Power BI to analyze global disaster events, economic losses, risk scores, and high-risk regions through interactive dashboards and geospatial visualizations.

1.DISASTER TYPE ANALYSIS

Objective

This analysis identifies which disaster categories occur most frequently and which types contribute the highest economic losses.

Business Importance

Insurance companies and catastrophe modeling firms use this information to understand where risk exposure is concentrated. High-frequency disasters may require different mitigation strategies compared to low-frequency but high-impact events.

Findings The output summarizes: Number of disaster events by type Total economic damage caused Average severity score SQL Output

2.COUNTRY RISK SUMMARY

Objective

Evaluate disaster risk at the country level by measuring event frequency, economic damage, and average severity.

Business Importance

Governments, reinsurers, and risk analysts can identify countries with the greatest catastrophe exposure and prioritize risk management resources.

Findings

The output provides:

Total disasters per country Total estimated economic losses Average disaster severity SQL Output

3.HIGH-RISK EVENTS ANALYSIS

Objective

Identify the most severe disaster events recorded in the dataset.

Business Importance

Extreme events often generate the largest insurance claims and humanitarian impact. Monitoring these events helps organizations improve preparedness and response planning.

Findings

The output highlights:

Event location Disaster category Severity score Economic damage Population affected SQL Output

4.EXECUTIVE RISK DASHBOARD

Objective

Measure the human impact of disasters across different countries.

Business Importance

Economic losses alone do not capture the full impact of catastrophes. Population exposure helps decision-makers allocate relief resources and understand vulnerability.

Findings

The output compares:

Population affected Economic losses Country-level disaster impact SQL Output

5.DISASTER RANKING ANALYSIS

Objective

Create a management-level summary of catastrophe risk metrics.

Business Importance

Senior executives require concise indicators rather than detailed event-level records. This summary combines multiple risk metrics into a single view.

Findings

The output includes:

Total disaster count Average severity Economic damage Population exposure SQL Output

6.GLOBAL DISASTER RISK MAP

Objective

Visualize catastrophe events geographically using an interactive map.

Business Importance

Spatial visualization enables analysts to quickly identify disaster clusters, regional patterns, and geographic concentrations of risk.

Features Country-level analysis Disaster-type filtering Interactive exploration Geospatial risk assessment Visualization

7.POWER BI EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD

Objective

Develop an executive dashboard for catastrophe risk monitoring.

Business Importance

Interactive dashboards transform raw data into actionable insights for analysts, insurers, governments, and risk managers.

Dashboard Components KPI Cards

Provide a quick overview of:

Total disaster events Total economic damage Average severity score Total event count Disaster Type Distribution

Shows the relative contribution of each catastrophe category.

Damage Analysis

Compares economic losses across countries.

Risk Ranking

Ranks countries based on catastrophe exposure.

Interactive Filters

Allow users to explore specific locations and disaster categories.
